COURTCTRL - Round Robin Court Grouping Display Fix

Fix:
- The Scoring & Standings match query now also retrieves match_schedules.court_id.
- Round Robin cards can therefore be grouped under their already-assigned active courts instead of disappearing.
- Category and bracket labels remain visible on each card.
- Court reassignment remains optional and collapsed under "Reassign court (optional)".
- Existing scoring controls are preserved.

Install:
1. Back up the current project folder and database.
2. Extract this ZIP.
3. Replace the pickleball_tournament folder in C:\xampp\htdocs\.
4. Refresh Scoring & Standings.

- Database/browser runtime test was not performed.
